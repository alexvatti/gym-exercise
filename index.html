Upper Chest

01 Upper Chest — Reference
BARBELL

Incline Barbell Bench Press

  • Bench angle: 30°–45°
  • Do not deliberately arch the back
  • Do not flare elbows outward
  • Controlled lowering
  • Lower slightly above nipple level
  • Press toward the upper-chest line
02 Upper Chest — Reference
DUMBBELL

Incline Dumbbell Press

  • Bench angle: 30°–45°
  • Hands controlled — no excessive flare
  • Raise/press through a controlled path
  • Approx. 90° arm position
  • Lower slightly above nipple level
  • Keep movement focused on upper chest
03 Upper Chest — Reference
REFERENCE

Upper Chest Technique

  • Use controlled range of motion
  • Avoid excessive elbow flare
  • Keep shoulder position stable
  • Do not over-arch the back
  • Control both lifting and lowering
  • Focus on chest rather than arms
